\section{\authorOne}

\subsection{What I did}
I was responsible for the implementation of dynamic arrays. I also focused heavily on understanding the template system, organizing our code, and structuring our files to ensure they were easily usable in test files. For this, I drew significant inspiration from the existing \texttt{map.h} file. Subsequently, I implemented Maps and Sets, covering both open and closed addressing strategies along with their various variants.

\subsection{What I learned}
I learned a great deal about data genericity, including techniques like hidden metadata, \texttt{void *} usage, and using macros to generalize our different types. I improved my ability to read and understand compiler errors---dealing with hundreds of them certainly helped! I also grasped the importance of optimizations and how different implementation choices can yield crucial time gains in benchmarks. Finally, I am left with a desire to have done more; I would have liked to explore further optimizations, such as array sizing strategies, and implement other data structures that I did not get to work on.
